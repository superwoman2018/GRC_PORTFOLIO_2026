# 05 — Risk Acceptance Documentation

**Framework:** Enterprise Risk Management
**Core skill demonstrated:** Communicating risk to business leaders — writing a
risk decision in language the accepting owner can actually understand and sign.

## What's in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `scenario.md` | The situation, and the reasoning behind each of the three risk acceptances |
| `deliverables/CloudNative_Risk_Acceptance_Register.xlsx` | Tracks all 3 accepted risks — owner, compensating controls, residual rating, review trigger |
| `deliverables/RA-001-Legacy-Finance-Admin-MFA.docx` | Fully worked risk acceptance form for RA-001 — the actual document a business owner would sign |

## How to read this

Start with the register for the overview, then open the RA-001 form to see what
a complete, individually-argued acceptance looks like end to end — executive
summary, business justification, risk assessment, compensating controls, review
trigger, and sign-off roles. RA-002 and RA-003 are documented in the register
only, in less depth, to show the range of situations that lead to a risk being
accepted rather than remediated. See `scenario.md` for the reasoning behind
each one and where I think the weakest of the three (RA-002) could be tightened.
